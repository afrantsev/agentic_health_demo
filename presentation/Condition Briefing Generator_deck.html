Why teams rely on it

Skip the desk research

No more hand-searching PubMed and trial registries before a meeting. You start with the landscape already mapped.

Check any claim in one click

Guidelines, facts and therapies link to their source record. A full source list shows which section used each one.

Current by design

Guidelines and reviews come from the last five years. Trials are limited to studies that are recruiting, active or about to start.

Clear about what is sourced

When a figure comes from general knowledge instead of a retrieved article, the briefing labels it that way. Trial counts appear only when the registry supplied them.

Written for decision-makers

Plain-language executive summaries, no marketing tone, and strategic notes on partners, trial sites and competitors.

Tested before you see it
